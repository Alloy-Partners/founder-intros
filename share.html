Share your LinkedIn connections

Alloy founders help each other raise. When another founder in the portfolio needs an intro to an investor you know, we'll ask you first, privately, and only you decide whether to make it.

How to get the file from LinkedIn

  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose Download larger data archive and click Request archive.
  3. LinkedIn emails you twice. The first email (minutes later) is a small archive without your connections. Wait for the second one, usually within 24 hours, then download that .zip.
  4. Drop the .zip below as it is. You don't need to unzip it.

What happens with it

  • This page reads Connections.csv from the archive in your browser and uploads only that file. Your messages, phone numbers and profile never leave your computer.
  • We keep only the people who work at investment firms. The rest of the list is deleted the same night, along with the file itself.
  • Nobody is contacted on your behalf. A path through you always starts with Alloy asking you, and you can say no.
  • Other founders never see your connections. Want something removed, or everything? Email mike@alloypartners.com and it's gone.
